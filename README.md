EXCEL DATA ANALYSIS PROJECT


TITLE : Restaurant Sales Data Analysis

DATASET DESCRIPTION :

      This project analyzes restaurant sales data to understand sales performance, product demand, payment methods, and manager-wise revenue.
The dataset contains 254 orders and includes the following fields:

•	Order ID
•	Date
•	Product
•	Price
•	Quantity
•	Purchase Type
•	Payment Method
•	Manager
•	City
•	Total Revenue
The analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel, including Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and KPI Cards.

BUSINESS QUESTIONS :

1. Which Product Are Ordered In The Highest Quantities ?
2. Which Payment Method Contributes The Highest Revenue ?
3. Which Is The Average Revenue Generated By Each Product ?
4. Which Manager Generates The Highest Total Revenue ?
5. Which Payment Method Is Used Most Frequently By Customer ?

KEY FINDINGS :

•	 Beverages have the highest quantity sold, with approximately 34,983 units, followed by Fries and Burgers.
•	Credit Card generates the highest revenue, contributing approximately €361,715.03, followed by Cash and Gift Card.
•	Burgers have the highest average revenue per order, at approximately €7,250.
•	Joao Silva generates the highest total revenue, at approximately €241,714.12, followed by Tom Jackson.
•	Credit Card is the most frequently used payment method, with 120 orders, followed by Cash with 76 orders and Gift Card with 58 orders


RECOMMENDATIONS :


•	Promote and maintain sufficient stock of Beverages because they have the highest sales
 quantity.

•	 Continue supporting convenient digital payment options, especially Credit Card payments,
 because they generate the highest revenue.

•	 Promote Burgers through combo offers and suitable promotions because they have the
 highest average revenue.
•	 Study the sales strategies of high-performing managers, particularly Joao Silva, and share effective practices with other managers.
•	Improve the performance of lower-selling products such as Sides & Other through combo offers and promotions.
